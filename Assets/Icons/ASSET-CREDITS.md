## Modified icons

`dark-return/chest-rune.svg` and `dark-return/chest-crown.svg` combine the chest by Delapouite with the rune stone or crown by Lorc. The composition adds a badge and a masked cutout. Both source contributors are credited above; the derivatives retain CC BY 3.0 attribution.

## Archived icons

Replaced, unused SVGs and previous inline navigation symbols are retained in [Archiv](Archiv/README.md). They are not loaded by the game. The original license remains applicable.
